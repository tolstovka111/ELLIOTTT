FAQ

What is 4real?

4real is my personal info-hub. It collects every official profile I have in one place, so you never have to guess which account is really mine.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Nothing here requires registration — just pick a platform from the Boards list on the front page and go.

Are all the links here official?

Yes. Every link on the front page points to an account I actually own. If you find a profile using my name that is not listed here, it is not mine.

How do I get in touch?

The fastest way is my Telegram channel. I read messages there more often than anywhere else.

Can I repost your pictures or avatars?

Sure, as long as you credit me and link back to this site. Please do not claim them as your own work and do not use them commercially without asking first.

How does the view counter work?

It counts unique visitors, not page loads. One address is counted once, so refreshing the page does not inflate the number. Addresses are stored only as a salted hash.
