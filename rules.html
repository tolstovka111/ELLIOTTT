Rules

These rules apply to my Telegram channel, comments and every other place my community gathers.

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, threats, racism or personal attacks.
  2. No spam, self-promotion or unsolicited advertising.
  3. No NSFW content, gore or anything illegal.
  4. Do not post personal information about anyone, including yourself.
  5. Do not impersonate me or pretend to speak on my behalf.
  6. Do not share scam links, fake giveaways or anything asking for logins or payments.
  7. Keep it on topic and do not derail conversations on purpose.
  8. Moderator decisions are final. Argue about them in private, not in the chat.

Breaking a rule gets you a warning. Doing it again gets you removed.
